# PRIVACY POLICY
**Effective Date:** November 24, 2026
**Last Updated:** November 24, 2026

At Rentivo, we recognize the importance of protecting personal information and respecting the privacy of individuals who interact with our platform.

This Privacy Policy explains how Rentivo ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, processes, shares, and protects personal information when you access or use our website, applications, software, and related services (collectively, the "Platform").

Rentivo is a cloud-based rental management platform designed to help vehicle rental businesses organize and manage their fleets, bookings, customers, contracts, rental operations, payments, and other business activities.

By accessing or using Rentivo, you acknowledge that you have been informed of the personal information processing activities described in this Privacy Policy.

Where consent is the applicable legal basis for a particular processing activity, we will obtain your consent as required by law.

## 1. SCOPE OF THIS PRIVACY POLICY
This Privacy Policy applies to personal information processed in connection with:

- Visitors to the official Rentivo website.
- Individuals who register for Rentivo accounts.
- Organization Owners and workspace administrators.
- Employees and other authorized Users of Rentivo workspaces.
- Individuals whose information is submitted by rental businesses through the Platform.
- Individuals who communicate with Rentivo through customer support or other official communication channels.
- Individuals who participate in Rentivo promotions, inquiries, or other business activities.
This Privacy Policy should be read together with the Rentivo Terms of Service.

## 2. DEFINITIONS
For purposes of this Privacy Policy:

**2.1. Personal Information** refers to any information from which an individual can be identified, whether directly or when combined with other information.

**2.2. Sensitive Personal Information** refers to personal information classified as sensitive under applicable data protection laws, including certain information relating to an individual's health, government-issued identifiers, and other legally protected information.

**2.3. Data Subject** refers to an individual whose personal information is collected or processed.

**2.4. Organization** refers to a rental business or other entity registered within Rentivo.

**2.5. Workspace** refers to an Organization's dedicated environment within the Platform.

**2.6. User** refers to an individual authorized to access an Organization's workspace.

**2.7. Customer Data** refers to information submitted, uploaded, generated, or maintained by an Organization or its authorized Users through the Platform.

**2.8. Processing** refers to any operation performed on personal information, including collection, recording, storage, retrieval, modification, disclosure, restriction, and deletion.

### 3.1. Account and Registration Information
When an individual registers for a Rentivo account, we may collect:

- Full name.
- Email address.
- Contact number.
- Login credentials and authentication information.
- Account registration date.
- Account status.
- Organization affiliation.

### 3.2. Organization Information

- Subscription status.

### 3.7. Communication Information

- Contact information.

Certain billing and transaction records may be retained for longer periods where necessary for accounting, legal, tax, regulatory, or dispute-resolution purposes.

Rentivo may retain certain information beyond ordinary retention periods when required by law or necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

## 12. DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS
Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 and other applicable laws, individuals may exercise certain rights concerning their personal information.

Subject to applicable legal conditions and limitations, these rights include:

### 12.1. Right to Be Informed
You have the right to know whether your personal information is being collected, processed, stored, or otherwise used.

### 12.2. Right to Access
You may request reasonable access to personal information concerning you that is being processed.

### 12.3. Right to Rectification
You may request the correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information.

### 12.4. Right to Erasure or Blocking
You may request the removal, blocking, or destruction of personal information where the applicable legal requirements are satisfied.

### 12.5. Right to Object
You may object to certain forms of personal information processing, subject to applicable legal conditions.

### 12.6. Right to Data Portability
Where applicable, you may request a copy of your personal information in a structured and commonly used electronic format.

### 12.7. Right to Damages
You may seek compensation for damages sustained due to inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, false, unlawfully obtained, or unauthorized use of personal information, subject to applicable law.

### 12.8. Right to File a Complaint
You may lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission if you believe your personal information has been mishandled or your data privacy rights have been violated.

### 12.9. Withdrawal of Consent
Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw your consent, subject to applicable legal conditions.

Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

The exercise of these rights may be subject to lawful limitations, including circumstances where information must be retained to comply with legal obligations or protect legitimate rights.
